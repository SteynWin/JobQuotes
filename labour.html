Cost is what the work costs you (wages and on-costs). Rate is what you charge the client, per hour (or per job for Cutters). The standard types (🔒) appear on every job at these rates. Rates are set only here and can't be changed on a job. Other types you add here, such as Sanding or Cleaning, can be added to a job with + Add other labour.
